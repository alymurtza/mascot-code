# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

This policy covers the Mascot app and the Mascot browser extension.

**In short: Mascot doesn't collect, store or share any information about you. Everything stays
on your own computer.**

## The browser extension

The extension hands the downloads you start in your browser to the Mascot app installed on the
same computer. To do that it uses:

- **The download's address, file name, size and the page it came from**, to pass the download
  to Mascot and to leave alone any site you've turned off.
- **Cookies for that download's address only**, when a download is handed to Mascot, so Mascot
  can fetch files from sites you're signed in to. They go only to the Mascot app on your
  computer.
- **Your settings** (on or off, sites to leave alone, what to do while Mascot is closed) and the
  list of downloads waiting for Mascot to open, saved in your browser on this computer. A
  waiting download is removed once it's handed over, sent to the browser or removed by you.

The extension talks only to the Mascot app on your own computer. It doesn't send anything to us
or anyone else, doesn't read the pages you visit (it only shows the current site's name when you open its button), and has no ads, analytics or tracking.

## The app

The Mascot app connects only to:

- **the servers you download from** (and, for torrents, other people sharing the same torrent),
  to download your files; and
- **GitHub** (github.com), to look for new versions of Mascot.

Your downloads, settings and history are stored only on your computer. There are no accounts,
ads, analytics or tracking.

## Selling and sharing

We don't sell, share or transfer any data, because we don't receive any.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
